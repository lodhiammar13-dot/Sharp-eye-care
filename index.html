Explore our services

01 / Everyday vision↗

Comprehensive eye exams

Get a closer look at your vision and overall eye health with a thorough eye examination.

What to expect +

An eye exam may include vision checks and an assessment of your eye health. Your clinician will explain what each step involves.

02 / Lens & focus↗

Glaucoma care

Screening and ongoing care to help monitor eye pressure and protect your vision.

Learn about glaucoma +

Glaucoma can affect vision gradually. Regular eye evaluations can help identify changes early and guide appropriate care.

03 / Clear vision↗

Cataract evaluation

Understand changes in clarity and explore whether cataracts may be affecting your sight.

About cataracts +

A cataract is clouding of the eye’s natural lens. An evaluation helps your eye care professional understand your symptoms and options.

04 / Little eyes↗

Children’s eye care

Gentle, age-aware eye care to support developing vision and growing confidence.

For growing eyes +

Children may not always notice vision changes. A child-friendly eye check can help assess how their vision is developing.

05 / Retinal health↗

Retina & diabetic eye care

Eye health monitoring for people living with diabetes and care for conditions affecting the retina.

Why retinal checks matter +

Diabetes can affect blood vessels in the retina. Routine monitoring helps your care team look for changes and discuss next steps.

Services and treatment suitability are determined by the ophthalmologist after an individual evaluation.

Good questions.

Want to know more about eye care or what to expect? Start here.

What happens during a comprehensive eye exam?+

Your eye care professional will ask about your vision and health, then perform relevant tests to assess your eyesight and eye health. The exact checks depend on your needs.

How often should I get my eyes checked?+

It depends on your age, health, symptoms and risk factors. An eye care professional can suggest an interval suited to you.

Do you provide care for children?+

Sharp Eye Care offers children’s eye care. Contact the clinic directly to confirm which services are available for your child.
